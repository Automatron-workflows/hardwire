# Community guidelines

Help people understand, try and improve Hardwire. Be specific, respectful and
generous with context. Questions and reports of confusing behavior are welcome.

Discuss ideas and evidence. Do not harass people, make personal attacks, publish
private information or flood discussions with promotion. Use synthetic or
permitted, redacted examples. Never include credentials or private customer data.

Keep claims accurate. A fixture is not an integration, a digest is not proof of
execution, and a star is not evidence of adoption. Credit other people's work
and respect the license of every contribution.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, redirect conversations, lock
threads or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and focused on keeping collaboration possible. Use GitHub's reporting
controls for abuse. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive defects.

Maintainers apply these expectations to themselves too. Support is voluntary;
the project does not promise a response time or production support.
